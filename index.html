Welcome to Buraaq

Education that nurtures the whole child.

We aim to nurture confident learners who are prepared for school, family and community life. Our learning approach brings together foundational literacy and numeracy, the wider primary curriculum, Qur’an recitation and Islamic studies.

We value respect, kindness, responsibility and a love of learning. Families are important partners in each learner’s progress.
