A healthy school life

Every child deserves a chance to play.

Sports give learners opportunities to cooperate, practise discipline, develop coordination and enjoy positive competition. Activities are organised according to available facilities, learner age and the school timetable.

TEAM SPORT

Football ⚽

Football encourages communication, fitness, teamwork and respect for teammates and opponents. Learners practise basic skills and team play.

  • Passing and ball control
  • Teamwork and fair play
  • Fitness and coordination
COURT SPORT

Netball

Netball helps learners build agility, spatial awareness, communication and decision-making in a team setting.

  • Passing and movement
  • Position awareness
  • Respectful competition
TEAM SPORT

Volleyball

Volleyball introduces learners to coordination, cooperation and quick thinking while they learn the fundamentals of the game.

  • Serving and passing
  • Communication
  • Balance and coordination
ALL-ROUND DEVELOPMENT

Games & Quran reciatation

Age-appropriate games and group activities give children a chance to stay active, make friends and discover new interests.

  • Relay and movement games
  • Group challenges
  • Participation for all
