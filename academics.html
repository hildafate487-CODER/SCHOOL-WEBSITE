Our learning approach

Two curricula, a balanced education.

Our school’s educational aim is to combine the English/primary curriculum with Islamic learning. Learners develop communication, numeracy, scientific thinking and problem-solving skills while learning about Qur’an recitation, Islamic studies, good manners and responsibility.

Teaching methods and subjects may vary by class. Contact the school to confirm the current timetable, curriculum coverage and assessment arrangements.

✓
English curriculumLiteracy, numeracy, science and broader primary learning.
✓
Islamic curriculumQur’an, recitation, Islamic studies and values.
✓
Continuous assessmentClass work, practice and feedback to support progress.
